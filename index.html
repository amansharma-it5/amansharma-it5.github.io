About Aman

Make the useful thing feel inevitable.

I’m Aman Sharma — a product-minded builder working across Android, web and automation. I like taking real-world friction, shaping it into a clear product surface, and tightening the edges until the system is honest about what it can and cannot do.

That means local-first data when privacy matters, explicit states when a service is unconfigured, and interfaces that make the next step obvious.
